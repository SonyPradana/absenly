# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming place for everyone, regardless of
experience, identity, or background. Harassment of any kind will not be
tolerated.

## Our standards

Behaviour that makes this project work:

- Being kind and patient, especially with beginners.
- Accepting constructive criticism and giving it in good faith.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.
- Respecting that maintainers have limited time.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone else's private information (doxxing).
- Trolling, derailing, or sustained off-topic argument.
- Any conduct that would be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to `sonypradana@gmail.com`. All reports will be
reviewed and investigated, and the maintainer will respond as appropriate.
Confidentiality will be respected. Maintainers may remove comments, close
threads, or ban contributors who break these rules.
